# Functions, Duties, and Responsibilities of the Intern
**Internship Journey: Development of the School Clinic Management System**

---

As an Information Technology intern, I was responsible for the full development and deployment of the School Clinic Management System. My primary function was to design, build, and maintain a web-based clinic system that digitized the school's manual medical record-keeping processes. This involved gathering requirements from the medical staff, creating UI/UX mockups, and developing both the front-end and back-end of the system using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL.

I was also responsible for implementing security features such as password hashing, SQL injection prevention, and role-based access control to ensure that sensitive patient data was properly protected. In addition, I developed key system modules including an inventory management system for medicines and medical supplies, an automated medical certificate generator for doctors, and a public online health form accessible to students and employees across the campus network.

Part of my duties included setting up and configuring the local network infrastructure, which involved router setup, static IP assignment, and physical LAN cable installation and maintenance for the faculty offices. I also managed the data migration process, encoding thousands of historical patient records from paper archives into the digital database, and conducted beta testing and debugging to ensure system stability.

Toward the end of my internship, I deployed the system to a live web server, configured SSL for secure HTTPS access, and conducted full user training and demonstrations for all system users including administrators, doctors, nurses, and staff. I also prepared a user manual to support the clinic's independent use of the system after my internship concluded.
